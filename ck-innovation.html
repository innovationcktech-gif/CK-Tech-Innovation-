Company Overview

CK Tech Innovation

Founded in 2015, CK Tech Innovation has been at the forefront of providing cutting-edge mobile technology solutions in Zambia. We specialize in mobile phones and accessories sales, along with comprehensive mobile and computer repair services.

Mission: To provide innovative, affordable, and reliable mobile technology solutions that enhance our customers' digital experiences.

Vision: To be the leading tech solutions provider in Zambia, recognized for excellence in service and innovation.

Core Values: Innovation, Quality, Reliability, Customer Care.

Our Story

2015

The Beginning

CK Tech Innovation was founded with a vision to bring quality mobile technology solutions to the Zambian market. Starting as a small kiosk in Lusaka, we focused on providing genuine accessories and reliable repair services.

2017

Expansion

Due to increasing demand, we expanded our operations to include mobile phone sales and computer repairs. Our team grew to include certified technicians with expertise in various mobile brands.

2019

Corporate Partnerships

We established partnerships with major mobile brands and began serving corporate clients, providing bulk device purchases and maintenance contracts for businesses and educational institutions.

2022

Digital Transformation

Embracing the digital age, we launched our e-commerce platform and enhanced our service capabilities to include software solutions and device customization services.
